## 📌 Overview

ReviveAI is an AI-powered Business Recovery Assistant that helps businesses analyze customer reviews, identify recurring issues, understand customer sentiment, and generate actionable recovery strategies.

Instead of manually reading hundreds of customer reviews, business owners receive an AI-generated business intelligence dashboard containing insights, trends, complaint analysis, executive summaries, and recovery recommendations within seconds.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Businesses receive valuable customer feedback every day, but manually analyzing reviews is:

- ⏳ Time-consuming
- 📉 Difficult to identify recurring issues
- ❌ Inefficient for understanding customer sentiment
- 📊 Lacking actionable business insights

As a result, businesses often fail to address customer concerns promptly, leading to poor customer retention and declining reputation.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

ReviveAI leverages **Google Gemini AI** to automatically analyze customer reviews and convert unstructured feedback into meaningful business intelligence.

The platform enables businesses to:

- 😊 Understand customer sentiment
- 📂 Identify complaint categories
- 📈 Detect recurring issues and trends
- 📊 Measure overall business health
- 📝 Generate executive summaries
- 💡 Receive AI-powered business recovery recommendations

## 🔮 Future Developments

We envision ReviveAI evolving into a complete AI-powered customer recovery platform.

### 🌐 Google Business Profile Integration

Businesses will be able to securely connect their **Google Business Profile**, allowing ReviveAI to automatically fetch customer reviews in real time without requiring manual CSV uploads.

---

### 💬 AI Customer Recovery

When the AI detects dissatisfied customers from negative reviews, ReviveAI will automatically:

- Generate personalized apology messages
- Suggest empathetic responses
- Create discount coupons or promotional offers
- Encourage customers to revisit the business

Our goal is to transform negative customer experiences into opportunities for customer retention and brand loyalty.
